# GAME VISION

**VARDHOLM** · *SURVIVE THE ISLAND. DISCOVER THE TRUTH.*
Premium portrait mobile mystery-adventure · iOS + Android · Unity 6 · 8–10 hour golden path

The canonical one-page pitch. Story detail lives in
[`design/01-story-bible.md`](design/01-story-bible.md) — **the naming authority for every proper
noun in this project.** Loop detail: [`design/06-core-game-loop.md`](design/06-core-game-loop.md).

## The pitch

> A disgraced marine acoustician, shipwrecked on an island that isn't on any chart, discovers a
> Bronze Age machine built to keep the ocean quiet — and the woman who has been repairing it
> alone for thirty-one years.

## Player fantasy

You are **Nadia Vesk**, 41, whose career ended when she deleted a raw data capture that turned out
to be real and cost nine people their lives. She chased that signal again, off-book, and the reef
took her boat.

The fantasy is not "stay alive" — survival is the first hour, not the game. It is **being the
analyst who finally refuses to simplify the data**. Her engine is not survival; it is *completion
of the record*. The emotional target is not fear but the specific dread of realising **someone is
still doing the work**: a swept floor, a re-roped bridge, fresh batteries in a dead man's radio.

## The loop

Explore → **Listen** → Observe → Collect → Analyse → Combine → Solve → Unlock → Discover → Survive

**Listening is the signature verb.** Raising the hydrophone resolves the world into a directional
spectrogram — simultaneously the navigation tool, the puzzle language, the accessibility answer to
portrait first-person's blind periphery, and the thesis of the story. *(It is the Act 2 mechanic;
the prologue rehearses its grammar — aim, sweep, isolate — on a radio dial.)*

## What makes it different

1. **The mystery is a maintenance problem.** Not a monster, not a curse. The final antagonistic
   pressure is an employment contract with a foundation that dissolved in 1994 and never told its
   last employee she could stop.
2. **Listening as mechanic, not metaphor.** Cheap to render, expensive to imitate.
3. **The ancients were competent, not mystical.** Engineers who organised a society around upkeep.
4. **Nothing supernatural is ever confirmed** — and never dangled as a tease.
5. **No combat, no creature threat, no weapons, ever.** The signature tool is a microphone.
6. **Built portrait-first**, not ported. Vertical is the axis the island is designed on.

## Audience

Adults 25–45 who finished *Outer Wilds*, *Return of the Obra Dinn*, *Firewatch* or *Myst* and were
never offered that experience natively on a phone. Readers of the journals. Secondary: the
abandoned-places / deep-sea nonfiction audience. **Explicitly not** the free-to-play hidden-object
shelf — the title strategy exists to avoid being shelved there.

## Sessions and length

Designed for **12–20 minute sessions**: one zone objective, one document, one combination. Every
session ends on a Field Slate entry, which is both a natural stopping point and the autosave
boundary. Supports 90-minute sittings unchanged. Golden path **8–10 h**; completionist ~14.

## Commercial model — locked (D-3)

**Free prologue → one-time full unlock.** No ads. No energy. No consumable currency. No
pay-to-win. No purchase-pressure timers.

The prologue is **30–45 minutes** and must deliver all eight beats before the unlock decision:
shipwreck → first exploration → first environmental puzzle → the Field Slate → radio repair →
mysterious transmission → first major reveal → entry into the next major area.

The unlock must read as **"continue the mystery"**, never "pay to keep playing."

## The five acts

| Act | Zone(s) | Player gains | Revelation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Survival | The Ribcage, Fernmaw | Crafting, fire, the Catchment | The gully is cut stone, graded — and the machete cuts are days old |
| 2 The Signal | Fernmaw, Sweatways, Rime Shoulder | **The hydrophone**, climbing, the Field Slate | Someone already tried to signal out, and failed |
| 3 The Camp | Fold Camp, Ash Throat | Electrical repair, the Reel Deck, the maintenance ledger | A maintained grave, and a radio dead since 1989 with new batteries |
| 4 The Ruins | The Combs, Substation Oleander | The Register, the damper console | Weir-Keeper is a title across six generations |
| 5 The Truth | The Quiet Room | **Nothing — the crafting HUD is stripped** | Hanne Lorvik, and the choice |

**The ending costs something either way.** *The Record* buys Nadia's absolution with other
people's safety; *The Relief* buys their safety with her absolution. No third option, no epilogue
saying which was right.

## Tone guardrails

Premium, cinematic, grounded, mysterious. **Not** cartoonish, **not** a hardcore survival sim,
**not** a free-to-play ad game. Palette: deep greens, dark blue, sunset orange, natural stone,
muted UI, gold for discoveries, red for danger.

**Identity is locked (D-5).** Never add combat, weapons, monster chasing, zombie mechanics,
crafting grind, base-building grind, or loot rarity.
