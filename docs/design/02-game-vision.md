# GAME VISION — ONE PAGE

**Working title:** VARDHOLM *(recommended; supersedes the working title "The Forgotten Isle" — see `docs/production/01-title-evaluation.md`)*
**Tagline:** SURVIVE THE ISLAND. DISCOVER THE TRUTH.
**Platform:** iOS + Android · Portrait · Unity 6 LTS / C# / URP
**Cites:** Story Bible v1.0 (`01-story-bible.md`)

---

## PLAYER FANTASY

> *You are the only person who will ever write down what happened here.*

You are **Nadia Vesk**, 41, a marine acoustician whose career ended when she deleted a raw data
capture that turned out to be real and cost nine people their lives. You chased that signal again,
off-book, and the reef took your boat.

The island you wash up on is not on any chart. The fantasy is not "stay alive" — survival is the
first hour, not the game. The fantasy is **being the analyst who finally refuses to simplify the
data.** You carry a hydrophone. You learn to read a mountain as a resonant body. And you slowly
establish that the ruins under the jungle are not ruins at all — they are the working parts of a
four-thousand-year-old machine whose product is *silence*, that a 1970s research programme broke
it, and that one 66-year-old woman has been holding the damage shut, alone and unpaid, since 1994.

The emotional target is not fear. It is **the specific dread of realising someone is still doing
the work** — a swept floor, a re-roped bridge, fresh batteries in a dead man's radio.

## THE LOOP

Explore → **Listen** → Observe → Collect → Analyse → Combine → Solve → Unlock → Discover → Survive.
The signature verb is **listening**: raising the hydrophone resolves the world into a directional
spectrogram, revealing water behind rock and machinery behind walls. It is simultaneously the
navigation tool, the puzzle language, the accessibility answer to portrait-FP's blind periphery,
and the thesis of the story. See `06-core-game-loop.md`.

## TARGET AUDIENCE

Premium-adventure players who finished *Myst*, *Firewatch*, *Return of the Obra Dinn* or *Outer
Wilds* and have never been offered that experience natively designed for a phone. Adults 25–45 who
read the journals. Secondary: the archaeology/deep-sea/abandoned-places nonfiction audience.
**Explicitly not** the free-to-play hidden-object shelf — the title strategy exists to avoid being
shelved there.

## DIFFERENTIATION

1. **The mystery is a maintenance problem.** Not a monster, not a curse, not a cover-up with a
   trigger finger. The antagonistic pressure is an employment contract with a foundation that
   dissolved in 1994 and never told its last employee she could stop.
2. **Listening as a mechanic, not a metaphor.** No other mobile adventure makes acoustics the
   primary sense. It is also cheap to render and expensive to imitate.
3. **The ancients were competent, not mystical.** The Tolo Vardh were engineers who organised a
   society around upkeep. No prophecy, no chosen one, no lost wisdom.
4. **Nothing supernatural is ever confirmed** — and never dangled as a tease. Every effect has a
   stated physical cause. This is a hard rule of the fiction, not a tone preference.
5. **No combat, no creature threat, no weapons, ever.** The signature tool is a microphone.
6. **Built portrait-first**, not ported. Vertical framing is the axis the island is designed on.

## SESSION LENGTH

Designed for **12–20 minute sessions**: one zone objective, one document, one combination. Every
session ends on a Field Slate entry, which is both a natural stopping point and the autosave
boundary. Supports 90-minute sittings without a difficulty or pacing change. Total golden path
**8–10 hours**; completionist ~14.

## COMMERCIAL MODEL

**Free prologue → one-time premium unlock.** The 30-minute prologue (`04-first-30-minutes.md`) is
the entire free product and ends on the unknown signal. No ads, ever. No pay-to-win, no energy
timers, no consumable IAP. Architecture supports Unity IAP, restore purchases and future expansion
unlocks (*The Lost City*, *Beneath the Island*) from Phase 1 — **but nothing is implemented until
Phase 15.** The price point is defended by the title, the icon and the first two screenshots.

## THE ONE-SENTENCE PITCH

*A disgraced acoustician, shipwrecked on an island that isn't on any chart, discovers a Bronze Age
machine built to keep the ocean quiet — and the woman who has been repairing it alone for
thirty-one years.*
