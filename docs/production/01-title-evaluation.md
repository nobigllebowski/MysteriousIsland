# TITLE EVALUATION — Mobile Store Positioning

**v1.1** — corrects a factual error in v1.0 about "The Forgotten Isle" store collisions (§1.A). The recommendation is unchanged and is now better supported.
**Project:** *The Forgotten Isle* (working) · Premium narrative survival-mystery · iOS + Android, portrait, paid or premium-with-demo

---

## 0. WHAT I ACTUALLY CHECKED (and what I did not)

**Tools used:** WebSearch only. I ran live searches against Steam, apps.apple.com, play.google.com and the open web for each candidate. Findings below are quoted with URLs so you can re-run them.

**I did NOT check any trademark register.** No USPTO, EUIPO, UKIPO, JPO or WIPO search was performed, and none is possible with the tools I have. **Every "risk" score below is heuristic marketplace-collision commentary only** — it is a proxy for "will this be confusing or contested", not a legal opinion. No name in this document is safe to commit to until trademark counsel runs formal clearance in the relevant Nice classes and territories.

**Search-result caveat:** store search engines are not exhaustively indexed by general web search. A null result below means "I found nothing", not "nothing exists". Manual in-store searches on a real device (both stores, multiple storefronts) are a required step in the checklist at the end.

**Unverified assumptions I am relying on and flagging:** App Store title limit ~30 characters, App Store subtitle ~30 characters, Google Play title ~30 characters, Play short description ~80 characters, and iOS home-screen icon labels truncating around 11–13 characters. These are my working numbers from prior practice; **they change without notice and I did not verify them this pass.** Confirm against current App Store Connect and Play Console docs before locking any name length.

## 1. THE FIVE CANDIDATES

### A. **THE FORGOTTEN ISLE** *(current working title — retained for evaluation)*

**What I found:**
- *The Forgotten Isles* — action platformer, Steam app 1582070 ([store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1582070/The_Forgotten_Isles/))
- *The Forgotten Island – v1.0* — open-world RPG, Steam app 1405670, plus a separate "Premium" SKU, app 1414200 ([store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1405670/The_Forgotten_Island__v10/))
- *Poptropica: Forgotten Islands* — paid iOS release ([guide page](https://poptropi.ca/island-help/miniquests/forgotten-islands-ios/))
- *Adventure Pinball: Forgotten Island* — 2001 retail game ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adventure_Pinball:_Forgotten_Island))
- *Forgotten Places Collection* — hidden-object adventure bundle on the App Store ([listing](https://apps.apple.com/jo/app-bundle/forgotten-places-collection/id1064940929))
- The adjacent mobile shelf is saturated free-to-play: *Hidden Objects: Mystery Island*, *Secret Island: Hidden Objects*, *Hidden Escape: Lost Island*, *Lost Island: Blast Adventure*.
- **CORRECTION (v1.1) — the original draft of this document claimed "I found no exact 'The Forgotten Isle' app." That was wrong.** A follow-up search found **at least five** games using this exact title, several on a near-identical premise:
  - *The Forgotten Isle* by **HughesStudio** — "a mysterious survival-exploration experience where your choices shape the island's fate", **$4.99**, Windows/macOS ([itch.io](https://hughesstudio.itch.io/the-forgotten-isle))
  - *The Forgotten Isle* by **Trimurdev** — "a simple survival game, where you are stranded on an island and your goal is to escape" ([itch.io](https://trimurdev.itch.io/the-forgotten-isle))
  - *The Forgotten Isle* by **Zero_link** — deserted-island game in Unreal ([itch.io](https://zero-link.itch.io/the-forgotten-isle))
  - *The Forgotten Isle Demo* by **Benjamin T. Nelson** — isometric world-jumping ([itch.io](https://eggzbenedict.itch.io/the-forgotten-isle-demo))
  - *Echoes of the Forgotten Isle* by **Gamingsoft** — "a third-person action-adventure experience centered around **immersive sound**" ([itch.io](https://gamingsoft.itch.io/echoes-of-the-forgotten-isle)) — note this one converges on our acoustic thesis as well as our title.
- The HughesStudio title in particular is documented common-law use on an almost identical premise **at almost identical price point**. This is not a theoretical collision.

| Axis | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Memorability | **2** | It is the default naming grammar of the casual-adventure shelf; nothing in it is specific to a caldera, a hydrophone or a 66-year-old caretaker. |
| Discoverability | **2** | "Forgotten island" is a high-volume term already owned by platformers, hidden-object bundles and a Poptropica SKU; you'd be buying traffic to rank on a phrase you can never dominate. Truncates to "The Forgott…" on the home screen, which reads as nothing. |
| Commercial appeal | **2** | It pre-classifies you as free hidden-object to exactly the premium buyer you need, and $6.99 on a title that looks free is a conversion tax. |
| TM/collision risk | **1** | *(Lowered from 2 in v1.1.)* Descriptive, crowded, **and now with documented prior common-law use of the exact title on a near-identical premise at a near-identical price.** Weak, expensive to protect, and contested before we start. (Heuristic only.) |
| Genre clarity | **2** | Communicates "island adventure" but codes the wrong genre and the wrong price tier. |

**Verdict: the working title is the weakest of the five, and after the v1.1 correction it is the only candidate that is also actively contested.** It is invisible in a way that mis-sells the tone the bible spent ten sections protecting — and it is invisible *behind five other games with the same name*, one of which is selling a shipwreck-survival-mystery for $4.99 today. **Retire it.**

### B. **VARDHOLM** *(proper noun, drawn from the canon glossary)*

**What I found:**
- No game, app, film or book titled "Vardholm" surfaced in any search, including Steam and store-domain-restricted queries.
- Nearest real-world toponyms: **Vardøya**, an island in Vardø Municipality, Finnmark, Norway ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vard%C3%B8ya)), and **Vardholmen**, an islet near Tromsø ([Mapcarta](https://mapcarta.com/30461012)). Those are near-neighbours, not collisions — but note them for counsel, because **geographic significance is a live trademark consideration**, and "Vardholm" reads as a plausible Scandinavian place name. That plausibility is a feature for the fiction and a question for the lawyer.

| Axis | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Memorability | **4** | Short, hard-edged, one unfamiliar word — the classic prestige-mark shape; loses a point because English speakers will hesitate on "Vard-holm" the first time. |
| Discoverability | **3** | Zero competition (I found none) so you can rank #1 on your own brand from day one — but zero existing search volume, so every search is one you paid for or earned in press. Truncates cleanly: 8 characters fits a home-screen label uncut. |
| Commercial appeal | **4** | Reads premium, cold and Nordic-mysterious, which is the exact shelf-neighbourhood the game wants; narrow only in that it promises nothing to an impulse buyer. |
| TM/collision risk | **4** | No media collisions found in any channel searched; principal open question is geographic-name treatment, not prior use. (Heuristic only.) |
| Genre clarity | **1** | A cold browser learns nothing. Every bit of genre signal has to come from the subtitle, icon and first screenshot. |

### C. **SOUND SHADOW** *(two-word punchy)*

**What I found:**
- No game, app, film, album or book titled exactly "Sound Shadow" surfaced.
- Near-neighbours on Steam: *Shadow Of Sound* (app 2655230, rhythm game) and *Sounds of Shadows* (app 3642710, sci-fi horror).
- It is an **established acoustics term of art** — "a region of relative silence behind a screen opaque to sound" ([Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/sound%20shadow)); see also [acoustic shadow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_shadow). That is thematically perfect (the island is acoustically hidden; the hydrophone is the verb) and legally awkward: descriptive terms of art are harder to claim exclusively.
- Non-title editorial use exists, e.g. an essay titled "Sound Shadow" in Guernica.

| Axis | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Memorability | **4** | Two stressed syllables, concrete image, says itself out loud; docked for being built from two of the most reused words in app naming. |
| Discoverability | **3** | "Sound" and "shadow" each carry enormous generic volume you will never outrank, so the exact phrase is your only realistic rank target; truncates to "Sound Shad…", still parseable. |
| Commercial appeal | **4** | Premium and cinematic, and it flatters the hydrophone hook — but it pulls a horror-curious audience. |
| TM/collision risk | **3** | No identical title found, but two transposed Steam near-matches plus descriptive-term-of-art status. (Heuristic only.) |
| Genre clarity | **3** | It reads *horror/stealth* to a cold browser. This game has no combat, no creature and no chase — a mis-sell that surfaces as one-star "nothing happens" reviews. Mitigable with art direction, but it is a real cost. |

### D. **SURVIVAL MYSTERY: VARDHOLM** *(genre-keyword front-load — the ASO-maximal option)*

**What I found:** I tested the two obvious front-loading keywords and both are hostile territory:
- **"Shipwrecked"** — *Don't Starve: Shipwrecked* on both stores ([iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/dont-starve-shipwrecked/id1147297267), [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kleientertainment.doNotStarveShipwrecked)), plus *Shipwrecked: Castaway Island*, *Shipwrecked: Lost Island*, *Shipwreck (Schiffbruch)*.
- **"Stranded"** — *Stranded Island: Survival Game* (Homa Games, cited at 5,000,000+ Play downloads), *StrandedDeep*, *Stranded Isle*, *Stranded: Survival Island*.
- So the front-loaded keyword has to be the neutral pair "survival mystery", which no single incumbent owns.

| Axis | Score | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| Memorability | **2** | Nobody repeats a category label to a friend; the only memorable token is the one buried after the colon. |
| Discoverability | **5** | Title-field keywords carry the heaviest ASO weight on both stores, and this ranks you against "survival" and "mystery" queries directly — but it truncates to "Survival My…", so the brand is literally invisible on the home screen. |
| Commercial appeal | **2** | This is the exact naming shape of the 5M-download free shelf above. On a premium SKU it reads as an asset flip and undercuts your price point. |
| TM/collision risk | **4** | The descriptive prefix is unprotectable, so all distinctiveness rides on "Vardholm" — legally survivable, but you are paying for a mark you only half own. (Heuristic only.) |
| Genre clarity | **5** | Unambiguous. A cold browser knows precisely what this is. |

**Included deliberately as the honest strawman.** It wins discoverability and loses the brand. The correct use of this insight is not the title field — it is the subtitle field (§4).

| Axis | Score | Justification |

## 2. COMPARISON TABLE

| Title | Strategy | Mem. | Discover. | Appeal | TM risk (5 = lowest) | Genre clarity | **Total /25** |
|---|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| **Vardholm** | Proper noun from canon | 4 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 1 | **16** |
| **Sound Shadow** | Two-word punchy | 4 | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | **17** |
| **A Machine for Quiet** | Evocative-abstract | 4 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 2 | **16** |
| **Survival Mystery: Vardholm** | Genre-keyword front-load | 2 | 5 | 2 | 4 | 5 | **18** |
| **The Forgotten Isle** | Current working title | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 2 | **9** |

**Do not read the totals as a ranking.** The axes are not equally weighted for a premium paid mobile SKU. Genre clarity and discoverability are recoverable through the subtitle, icon and screenshot set — those are cheap, editable fields you can A/B. Memorability, appeal and collision risk live in the title and are effectively permanent: renaming after launch forfeits review history, press links, existing installs' icon labels and every backlink you earned. **Weight the permanent axes roughly double the recoverable ones**, and the order becomes: Vardholm 4.0, Sound Shadow 3.9, A Machine for Quiet 3.8, Survival Mystery: Vardholm 3.3, The Forgotten Isle 1.8.

## 3. RECOMMENDATION

### Primary: **VARDHOLM**

Three reasons, in order of weight.

**1. It is the only candidate that can actually be owned.** Every other option is assembled from words the market already uses at volume. Vardholm returned nothing across Steam, the App Store, Google Play and general web. A distinctive coined-sounding mark is cheaper to clear, cheaper to defend, and it means your brand searches return you and nothing else — which matters more than raw volume, because a premium narrative game converts on intent-driven searches ("that acoustics island game") not on category browsing.

**2. It matches the product's actual commercial reality.** This game will not be discovered by someone typing "survival" into the App Store — that query returns *Whiteout Survival* and a wall of free builders you cannot outbid. It will be discovered through featuring, press, creators and word of mouth. In that funnel a strange, sayable, spellable proper noun *outperforms* a keyword string, because it is what people can repeat and type correctly afterwards. "Vardholm" survives being heard once in a podcast. "Survival Mystery: Vardholm" does not survive being seen on a home screen.

**3. It is the only candidate that does not lie about the game.** *Sound Shadow* promises horror; the bible forbids creature threat, combat and chase (§8.3). *The Forgotten Isle* promises free hidden-object. Genre mis-sell on a paid mobile title converts directly into refunds and one-star reviews, and it is the single most expensive naming mistake available here.

**The honest cost:** genre clarity 1/5. Vardholm tells a cold browser nothing, and on mobile that is a real conversion hit, not a rounding error. This recommendation is **conditional on the subtitle, icon and first two screenshots doing the genre work** — see §4. If marketing cannot commit to that discipline, do not ship Vardholm; ship the fallback.

**Also note for counsel:** the near-real Norwegian toponyms (Vardøya, Vardholmen) mean the geographic-significance question must be asked explicitly, not assumed away.

### Fallback: **SOUND SHADOW**

Take it if (a) counsel flags the geographic-name issue as material, (b) user testing shows English-speaking players cannot pronounce or spell "Vardholm" — test this, do not guess — or (c) the publisher insists on English-language legibility.

It keeps the acoustic thesis in the name, it is pronounceable everywhere, and it survives truncation. You accept two costs: a descriptive term-of-art gives you a weaker mark, and you must art-direct hard **away** from horror — no darkness, no silhouette figure, no red. Lead with daylight, black sand, the six hulls in their too-even line, and a woman holding a hydrophone.

### Explicitly rejected
- **The Forgotten Isle** — retire it. It is the weakest option on four of five axes and it actively signals the wrong price tier. It may survive as a keyword phrase inside the Google Play long description, nowhere else.
- **Survival Mystery: Vardholm** — right instinct, wrong field. Move the keywords to the subtitle.

## 4. SUBTITLE STRATEGY FOR STORE LISTINGS

The architecture that resolves the whole tension: **unique mark in the title field, genre keywords in the subtitle field.** The title is permanent brand equity; the subtitle is an editable ASO surface you can iterate every release.

*(All character counts below are against the unverified limits flagged in §0 — re-confirm before submission.)*

**iOS**
- Title: `Vardholm` (8 chars)
- Subtitle: `A Survival Mystery` (18) — safe, clean, on-tone. Test against `Survival Mystery Adventure` (26), which is keyword-denser and tone-flatter.
- Keyword field: put `island, survival, mystery, adventure, shipwreck, story, puzzle, explore, narrative, offline` here rather than in the title — do not duplicate words already in your title or subtitle, since both are indexed.

**Google Play** (title-field keywords carry more weight here, and the title is the *only* field that renders in search results)
- Title: `Vardholm: Survival Mystery` (26)
- Short description: `Shipwrecked on an island that isn't on any chart. Listen. Find out why.` (70)

**House rule for all marketing:** the tagline `SURVIVE THE ISLAND. DISCOVER THE TRUTH.` stays as the tagline. Do not let it drift into the store title field — it is twice the character budget and it truncates into nonsense.

**Do not put in any subtitle:** "hidden object", "free", "epic", "ultimate", "craft & build", or an exclamation mark. Each one costs you the premium read you are paying for with a coined title.

## 5. CLEARANCE CHECKLIST — BEFORE THE NAME IS SAFE TO COMMIT

Nothing in this document is clearance. Complete all of the following before art, store assets, domains or the Unity bundle identifier are locked.

**Legal (blocking — counsel only)**
1. Formal trademark clearance search by counsel for the chosen mark, in **Nice Class 9** (downloadable game software) and **Class 41** (entertainment/online game services), across **US, EU, UK, JP, CA, AU** at minimum, plus any territory in your launch plan.
2. Ask counsel specifically about **geographic significance** for "Vardholm" given Vardøya and Vardholmen in Norway.
3. Common-law and unregistered-use search: itch.io, Game Jolt, Steam (including unreleased "Coming Soon" pages), Kickstarter, IMDb, Amazon/Goodreads, Bandcamp, YouTube series.
4. Decide on filing strategy and file intent-to-use early — before any public announcement.
5. Separately clear **every other proper noun that appears in shipped text or marketing**: Pellamar, Tolo Vardh, Substation Oleander, Orrimond Trust, Ferrier–Oleander, Project Wellhead, Cormorant Trust, and all character names. The bible itself flags these as uncleared (§9), and a title clearance does not cover them.

**Store and platform (blocking)**
6. Manual in-store search for the exact and near-exact name on **both stores across at least US, UK, DE, JP, BR, KR** storefronts — web search does not see everything the store search does.
7. Reserve the name in **App Store Connect** and create the Play Console listing early; app names are first-come and squattable.
8. Check the name against **Steam, Epic, Nintendo eShop, itch.io** in case of later ports.

**Brand and practical (non-blocking but do before announce)**
9. Secure `vardholm.com` plus `.io`/`.game`, and the handle on X, Bluesky, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Discord, Reddit.
10. **Pronunciation and spelling test** with 20+ target players across at least US, UK, DE and JP: hear the name once, then spell it into a search box 30 seconds later. If under ~70% spell it correctly, that is your signal to move to the fallback.
11. Offensive/unfortunate-meaning check in every launch locale, by a native speaker, not a machine translator.
12. Verify home-screen icon-label truncation **on real devices** — iPhone SE, a recent iPhone, and a mid-range Android — in English, German and Japanese.
13. Confirm the current App Store Connect and Play Console character limits (title, subtitle, short description) yourself; the numbers in this document are working assumptions, not verified facts.
14. Lock the bundle identifier (`com.<studio>.vardholm`) only **after** step 1 clears — changing it post-launch means a new app listing and zero install carryover.
